# Relationship Lifetime Value

### From selling intelligence to helping people create the next frontier;

**The durable opportunity may be larger than selling more inference.** A provider can help people turn intelligence into discoveries, enterprises and shared knowledge, then sustain relationships as those people become mentors, collaborators, customers or voluntary benefactors. Andrew Fai proposes a university-inspired ecosystem around this idea. Oil is a useful analogy for differentiated capability; the university is a useful analogy for institutional purpose. Neither establishes a business case. The aim is an AI native Renaissance whose creators retain agency and recognition, while OpenAI earns credit and sustainable returns for enabling their work.

---

## Oil describes grades; electricity describes availability

Oil helps explain that one commodity label can hide different grades and refined products. Intelligence can likewise be offered with different capability, reasoning effort, latency, reliability and specialization. A demanding scientific workflow may need a different service from a routine administrative task.

But these differences do not form a single quality ladder. More reasoning can cost more without improving a particular task. Capability must be demonstrated on relevant work. Unlike a barrel of oil, knowledge can be copied and reused, while each act of inference still consumes resources. Electricity remains useful for describing availability and metering; oil is useful for describing differentiated inputs. The proposal needs both intuitions without making either literal.

**Sell an understandable capability for a task, with a visible price and evidence of fitness.** A future meter should distinguish the resources charged from the outcomes a user hopes to achieve.

## A customer can become a pioneer

A transaction ends when useful work is delivered. A relationship can continue as the person learns, builds a product, develops a research method, starts a company or helps someone else succeed. Those activities can generate several kinds of value:

| Form of value | What could emerge | How it might return to the ecosystem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service value | Useful work completed reliably | Continued purchases at an understandable price |
| Knowledge value | Methods, evaluations and reusable findings | Voluntary publication or agreed licensing |
| Network value | Mentors, collaborators and trusted referrals | Participation that helps others succeed |
| Enterprise value | New companies and productive workflows | Separately negotiated commercial partnerships or investments |
| Public value | Education, scientific progress and wider access | Shared resources, fellowships and voluntary giving |

These are **different forms of value, not automatically additive revenue**. The same successful project can appear in several rows. Counting it repeatedly would exaggerate the opportunity. Nor does supplying a model automatically create a claim over the customer's discoveries, data or company.

Relationship Lifetime Value is therefore a lens for evaluation before it is a financial formula: what can each party create, contribute and retain through a sustained, voluntary relationship?

## The university mindset

The useful university analogy is an institution that combines teaching, research, infrastructure, networks and support across a person's life. In this proposal, its practical equivalents could include accessible learning resources, research fellowships, founder support, shared evaluation tools and a community of experienced practitioners who help newcomers.

A person might begin with a small project, become a skilled builder, create a successful enterprise, then support future participants. That return could be mentorship, open research, ordinary commercial spending or a voluntary gift. It should remain possible to benefit without becoming a donor or an investee.

Altruism becomes credible through choices: publishing useful resources, allocating some support to work with broad social value, and making participation worthwhile even where no equity return is likely. The commercial question is whether the institution can sustain that support; goodwill alone does not pay for inference or long-term research.

OpenAI's [Academy announcement](https://openai.com/global-affairs/openai-academy/) already describes training, credits and community support. The Startup Fund's [2022 Converge announcement](https://openai.fund/news/introducing-converge) combines founder programming with equity investment. These are precedents to acknowledge, not inventions claimed here. The proposed extension is a coherent relationship across access, learning, discovery and voluntary reciprocity. The ownership and financial beneficiaries of any investment must be established for its specific vehicle; the OpenAI name alone does not answer that question.

## Three distinct channels for sustainability

**Service payments** fund a defined service. They need intelligible prices, measurable delivery and viable costs.

**Voluntary gifts** support a stated public-benefit purpose. They should not purchase preferential credit decisions, investment access or ownership of someone else's work. The prototype's Foundation contribution remains separate from the service bill and provider economics. No official donation channel or tax treatment is established here.

**Equity partnerships** would be individually negotiated arrangements for companies that want capital or substantial support. They involve investment risk, dilution and conflicts that cannot be treated as ordinary loyalty rewards. Future venture outcomes should not be used to hide an uneconomic service offering.

OpenAI describes a Foundation and a separately constituted Group PBC under nonprofit control. That is relevant institutional context, not evidence that this proposed program exists. The appropriate entity, resources and terms for any real arrangement would need to be established independently. [Source](https://openai.com/our-structure/).

## Physical AI and biotechnology make the idea consequential

Potential outputs extend beyond documents and software. Robotics research, experimental datasets and candidate molecules could become part of the value created. Their usefulness would depend on physical testing, reproducibility and domain expertise. A generated candidate is not a validated discovery, an effective treatment or a proven product.

“Powered and shielded by GPT” is most credible when translated into concrete support: engineering tools, evaluation, security assistance and access to qualified collaborators. GPT cannot promise scientific correctness, legal immunity, commercial success or protection from every failure. A serious ecosystem would make those support boundaries explicit.

## Recognition should follow contribution

OpenAI could earn substantial recognition through infrastructure that demonstrably helps people succeed. Attribution in research, transparent case studies and voluntary acknowledgments would make that contribution visible. Preassigning a majority of credit would diminish the scientists, founders and communities whose work makes the ecosystem valuable.

**The more credible ambition is to become indispensable to people's progress through usefulness and trust.** Participants should retain meaningful choice, including the ability to leave. Continued participation would then be stronger evidence of relationship value than retention created by dependence.

## A small beginning for a large ambition

Start with a bounded cohort and one task domain. Measure useful outputs, participant understanding, support costs, retention and willingness to recommend. Record failed projects as well as successes. Evaluate mentorship and shared research separately from purchases, gifts and investments. Compare participants with an appropriate alternative before attributing their progress to the program.

An AI native Renaissance is an aspiration, not a forecast. Relationship Lifetime Value gives it a practical question: **what would we build differently if we expected to grow alongside the people whose potential we help unlock?**
