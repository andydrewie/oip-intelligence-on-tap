## Read claims at their actual strength

The tariff is an adopted design choice. The oil and university comparisons are analogies. Better retention, more useful access and successful ventures are hypotheses. An AI native Renaissance and democratizing superintelligence are ambitions. None should be presented as a measured result.

The prototype's cost inputs remain unknown until supplied. A company-level financial ratio cannot fill that gap. The [economics review](ECONOMICS.md) reproduces the peer figures and explains their limits.

## Preserve the source record

The archived historical corpus is unchanged. Generated editorial chapters and newer repository essays are separate from that record. Neither missing speaker labels nor exact times should be invented. Start with [llms.txt](../public/llms.txt), select one relevant chapter, then retrieve a bounded source passage.

[Fresh publication checks](../review/github-presentation/REVIEW.md) and [historical interaction checks](../review/state-machines/REVIEW.md) describe different evidence. Unperformed physical-device and accessibility checks remain unperformed.
