# Intelligence on Tap: the proposal

**Explore a relationship that grows with what people create.** An optional $20 membership with metered intelligence is the entry point. Compounding loyalty and voluntary partnerships extend the idea from buying useful work to sustaining a community of pioneers. The prototype implements a billing rule; the relationship and ecosystem effects remain proposals for evaluation.

---

## The problem worth testing

A person's demand for intelligence can vary substantially between a quiet month and a demanding project. The hypothesis is that a comprehensible, optional usage-based path could support that variation while preserving fixed plans for people who prefer them. A lower predictable commitment could help some people; an unfamiliar settlement rule could confuse others. Both possibilities belong in the evaluation.

OpenAI already offers usage credits for eligible features. The proposal therefore asks how billing, loyalty and support could work together, rather than claiming that flexible usage itself is new. OpenAI Academy and the Startup Fund's Converge announcement also provide precedents for learning, community and founder support. See the dated [sources guide](SOURCES.md).

## Three layers; different levels of evidence

| Layer | Proposed value | Present status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access and billing | Optional $20 base, metered usage, visible tier settlement | Working receipt, formula, validation and regression tests |
| Compounding relationship | Benefits reflecting spend, tenure and reliability | Hypothesis; benefits and causal outcomes require evaluation |
| Pioneering ecosystem | Research support, mentors, voluntary giving and separately negotiated ventures | Conceptual extension; no live program or investment offer |

The [Relationship Lifetime Value essay](RELATIONSHIP-LIFETIME-VALUE.md) explains why a university-inspired ecosystem may be a useful ambition. Its success would depend on helping people achieve meaningful outcomes, including work whose value is public, scientific or cultural rather than commercial.

## What the prototype makes concrete

The receipt applies the adopted settlement rule to $20 plus metered usage. Named tier boundaries create both discounts and upward adjustments. The [economics review](ECONOMICS.md) exposes the exact formula and consequential examples, including the jump from a $350 subtotal to $350.01. These are intentional features of the current proposal, available for explicit challenge.

The provider worksheet begins with unknown costs. A person must enter and review allocations for the current usage before interpreting a balance. A voluntary Foundation contribution stays separate from service revenue, loyalty and commercial economics. The advanced plan comparison uses fictional units and declared assumptions.

No application connects personal accounts, takes payment, decides creditworthiness, chooses investments or synthesizes molecules.

## A path to learning

1. **Understand:** test whether people can predict the bill, explain boundaries and recover from mistakes.
2. **Simulate:** compare alternatives at matched workload, delivered quality and capacity, including adverse demand.
3. **Evaluate:** study useful work completed within a chosen budget, with separate results for newcomers and established users.
4. **Consider a pilot:** define a real meter, spending controls, operational costs and participant terms before offering a paid service.

These are proposed steps, not completed studies. OpenAI or another evaluator could adopt one useful part without adopting the whole system.

## The ambition

“Democratizing superintelligence” expresses a direction. This repository cannot establish that superintelligence exists here or that its benefits have been widely distributed. A useful near-term test is more concrete: can more people complete valuable work within budgets they understand and choose?

An AI native Renaissance would be an outcome of many people's work. Andrew offers this proposal in the hope that OpenAI can become an increasingly effective enabling institution, with its contributions visibly and fairly recognized.
