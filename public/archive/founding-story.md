# The Uber inspiration behind the bill

Status: Andrew’s personal inspiration, reported during the follow-up discussion after Pasta Board. This is an editorial account, not a verbatim quotation or an Uber product claim.

Tibo’s question about a $50 Pro plan prompted Andrew’s reply. As a ChatGPT Pro and Uber One subscriber, Andrew saw a familiar pattern: a membership, usage as you need it, and a clear bill at the end.

His proposal: a $20 ChatGPT base plus metered intelligence. Uber’s end-of-ride bill and optional tip inspired the checkout sequence: settle the usage bill first, then choose whether to donate to the OpenAI Foundation.

It meets the restaurant intuition already in the original discussion: order according to your appetite, settle transparently, support voluntarily.

The membership comparison is an analogy, not a statement of Uber One pricing or an endorsement. Uber tips go to drivers. This concept borrows the checkout sequence while naming a different destination: the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation, to support AI that benefits humanity. The Foundation pathway is proposed; no donation program or tax eligibility is promised.
