3. Strengthen the philosophical foundation of “Loyalty should compound.”

[p-6468](/archive/source.html#p-6468)

I would actually keep your original sentence.

[p-6514](/archive/source.html#p-6514)

Loyalty should compound.

[p-6539](/archive/source.html#p-6539)

It already has the compression and elegance you prefer.

[p-6595](/archive/source.html#p-6595)

But behind that statement, I see three different forms of compounding.

[p-6666](/archive/source.html#p-6666)

Economic compounding

[p-6687](/archive/source.html#p-6687)

Continued loyalty accumulates tangible benefits: compute credits, discounts, flexibility, and privileges that customers can actually use.

[p-6825](/archive/source.html#p-6825)

Relational compounding

[p-6848](/archive/source.html#p-6848)

A long-standing customer relationship creates opportunities for trust, personalization, and mutual understanding. Reliability must still be demonstrated rather than assumed.

[p-7022](/archive/source.html#p-7022)

Intelligence compounding

[p-7047](/archive/source.html#p-7047)

With the user’s permission, an agent can accumulate useful contextual knowledge, workflow familiarity, and preferences over time, becoming more useful without requiring the user to explain everything again.

[p-7254](/archive/source.html#p-7254)

The third dimension is perhaps the most philosophically significant.

[p-7323](/archive/source.html#p-7323)

In traditional subscriptions, loyalty generally accumulates economic rewards.

[p-7401](/archive/source.html#p-7401)

In an AI-native subscription, loyalty could also accumulate continuity of intelligence.

[p-7489](/archive/source.html#p-7489)

Your agent becomes increasingly familiar with your world. The value of the relationship may therefore grow beyond the computational resources purchased.

[p-7642](/archive/source.html#p-7642)

But there is a counterweight: portability. A company shouldn’t make customers feel imprisoned by an agent’s accumulated memory. User-owned context and the ability to export it are necessary to keep this mutually beneficial.
