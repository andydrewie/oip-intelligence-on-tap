Pricing formula & example bills

Exact rule adopted for this concept. Independently proposed by Andrew; not an official OpenAI tariff.

M is the metered charge in USD, excluding the $20 base. S is the subtotal; B(S) is the bill before any donation. All equation amounts are USD.

S=20+MS=20+M

B(S)={2020≤S≤6010060<S≤150200150<S≤350500350<S≤50050⌊S+2550⌋S>500B(S)=\begin{cases} 20, & 20\le S\le60,\\ 100, & 60<S\le150,\\ 200, & 150<S\le350,\\ 500, & 350<S\le500,\\ 50\left\lfloor\dfrac{S+25}{50}\right\rfloor, & S>500. \end{cases}

Scroll sideways if the full equation is not visible.

⌊x⌋\lfloor x\rfloor means round x down to a whole number. The +25 is half a $50 step.

At $60, $150 and $350, the midpoint settles lower. Above $500, halfway settles upward: $524.99 → $500; $525 → $550.
