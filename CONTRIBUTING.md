# Contributing

**Help make a claim clearer, an experiment stronger or an interaction more reliable.** Researchers, finance practitioners, founders and developers are all welcome.

A useful first contribution could be a counterexample to the tariff, a clearer explanation of a boundary, a study design, an accessibility finding or a reproducible defect. Use the [issue forms](https://github.com/andydrewie/oip-intelligence-on-tap/issues/new/choose) and explain what evidence would change your conclusion.

## Claims and concepts

Read [PROJECT_GUIDANCE.md](PROJECT_GUIDANCE.md) before changing behavior or claims. Keep facts, analogies, assumptions and hypotheses distinct. Cite dated primary sources where possible. Preserve the historical source corpus; update generated editorial material through its generator.

The adopted tariff and separation of service payments from Foundation donations are intentional. Propose conceptual changes explicitly. Relationship Lifetime Value includes voluntary knowledge sharing, mentorship and partnership; it does not establish ownership of a user's data, discoveries or company.

## Implementation changes

Explain the concrete problem, resulting behavior and evidence used to verify it. For interactions, describe initial state, action, guard, visible result, recovery and persistence. Check affected inputs across typing, calculation, sharing, refresh and reset. Review narrow and desktop layouts, including enlarged text.

Before a pull request:

1. Run the relevant checks in the [development guide](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
2. Exercise the changed interaction and an interrupted or error path in a browser.
3. Record what was actually tested and remaining device or accessibility gaps.
4. Update affected contracts and regenerate the archive, report and source ZIP when needed.
5. Keep private records and credentials out of code, screenshots and shared scenarios.

A pull request does not deploy the public site. Respectful disagreement and well-supported criticism are welcome; address the proposal and evidence rather than speculating about people's motives.
